## Walk 1: Boys hostels → Visvesvaraya → Chanakya (NR 0:00–4:20)
1. Boys hostel blocks (cream) → **Automobile Workshop** (roller shutter) → **Café Frisco** kiosk → fenced **construction yard** (open gravel ground). NR 0:06–1:02
2. Road along a red sheet fence, climbing toward **Visvesvaraya** (Petroleum & Civil). NR 1:06–1:38
3. At Visvesvaraya's west end: low **red-roofed annex** + green railings, then **switch-back stairs and ramps** with steel handrails and red/white parapets. NR 1:40–2:10
4. **Elevated ramp/walkway** on to Visvesvaraya's upper level; long brick facade alongside. NR 2:10–2:58
5. **Covered skywalk (bridge)** joins Visvesvaraya at an upper floor; it crosses the loop road at the west end (red strip in the satellite image). NR 3:02, DR, V6 8:38 ("bridge se through hai")
6. Down the road with **yellow-black kerbs** to **Chanakya** (Electrical & Electronics); green spiral **fire stairs** on the brick blocks. NR 3:06–3:58

## Walk 4: Chanakya → Vivekananda → Sarabhai → Raman → Bhabha → Annapurna (NR 24:00–54:40, BH)
1. Into **Chanakya** (Saraswati statue, inner atrium courtyards); out the back on to a lane between brick blocks with stone retaining walls. NR 25:18–32:10

5. Down the slope, stone retaining wall → **Raman Hostel** (cream, stepped balconies) → **basketball courts** and sheds. NR 45:00–46:38
6. **Bhabha Hostel** (pink + cream, curved entrance) → **Annapurna** boys mess (red brick + cream bands, front stairs). NR 48:38–51:02, BH 1:01–1:25, 5:15

8. **Sports Ground**: courts, cricket nets, tents, red-brick boundary; cream hostel with **green steel fire-stair tower**; **gravel path** along palms; **Amphitheatre** (semicircular steps around a court, enclosed by stepped-balcony hostels); **Gym & Tuck Shop** at the bottom of stairs by the road. BH 3:21–4:45
